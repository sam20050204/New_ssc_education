================================================================================
DATABASE INTEGRATION:
================================================================================

Source: FeePayment Model
Fields Used:
  - receipt_no: Unique payment receipt identifier
  - amount: Amount paid
  - payment_date: When payment was made
  - payment_mode: How payment was made (Cash, UPI, Card, Bank Transfer)
  - remarks: Additional notes about payment
  - paid_before_this: Amount paid before this payment
  - remaining_after_this: Remaining balance after this payment

Relationship: FeePayment → AdmittedStudent (via ForeignKey)
